Our Story

Connecting Ethiopia to its limitless potential.

In 2021, Safaricom Ethiopia embarked on a historic journey, becoming the first private telecommunications operator in the nation's history. We did not just arrive to build a network; we arrived to help awaken the digital potential of over 120 million people. Backed by a powerful global consortium, we began laying thousands of kilometers of fiber-optic cable and erecting state-of-the-art towers across the country, building a world-class infrastructure entirely from the ground up.

We operate on a single, unwavering belief: access to the internet and digital finance is a fundamental human right, not a luxury. Our mission is to democratize connectivity. By providing affordable, blazing-fast 4G and 5G networks, we are actively dismantling the geographic and economic barriers that have historically held back innovation.

Today, our story is no longer just about telecommunications. Through the rollout of M-PESA, we are revolutionizing financial inclusion, allowing small business owners in regional markets to trade globally from the palm of their hands. Our story is still being written every single day, fueled entirely by the unstoppable ambition, resilience, and brilliance of the Ethiopian people. We are Safaricom Ethiopia, and we are just getting started.

Our Journey

2021: The Historic License

After a highly competitive global bid, the Global Partnership for Ethiopia (led by Safaricom) was officially awarded the first-ever private telecommunications license in Ethiopia's history. The foundation is laid.

2022: The First Signal

Safaricom Ethiopia officially switches on its network. Starting in Dire Dawa and rapidly expanding to Addis Ababa and beyond, the first independent 4G data and voice calls are successfully transmitted across the country.

2023: The M-PESA Revolution

Safaricom launches M-PESA in Ethiopia, bringing world-class mobile financial services to millions. The platform instantly begins transforming how Ethiopians send money, pay bills, and run local businesses.

Today: The 5G Future

We are aggressively expanding our fiber-optic footprint and rolling out cutting-edge 5G enterprise solutions, while powering our towers with green solar energy to ensure a sustainable digital future for the next generation.
